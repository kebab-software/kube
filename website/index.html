About

Music should be simple.

Kube was born from frustration. Official music apps have become bloated, privacy-invasive, and resource-heavy. We believe you shouldn't have to sacrifice your privacy or device performance just to listen to music.

Powered by Invidious, an open-source alternative frontend to YouTube, Kube gives you access to millions of tracks while keeping your data completely private. No accounts, no tracking, no compromises.

"The best interface is the one you don't notice."

Ready to listen?

Download Kube and experience music the way it should be.
